4. Transaction Limits

Maximum transaction amount: $2,000 USD

Each individual transaction submitted through GateXchange must not exceed $2,000 USD unless otherwise approved after additional review.

Customers may submit multiple transactions. However, each individual transaction must remain within the applicable $2,000 limit.

If a customer wishes to process an amount greater than $2,000, the customer must clearly state the purpose of the transaction and may be required to provide additional information or documentation before GateXchange decides whether the transaction can be accepted.

Splitting a transaction into multiple requests for the purpose of avoiding verification, transaction limits or other controls may result in the transaction being delayed, rejected or reviewed further.

Customers should confirm the final rate and applicable charges with GateXchange before authorizing a transaction.

Processing times depend on the payment service, transaction verification, availability of funds and other factors.

Standard Transactions
